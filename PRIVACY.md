# Privacy policy for Akki Image Converter

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Akki Image Converter is provided by akki.lu. It converts and resizes images locally on your Windows PC.

## Images and local settings

The app reads the images and folders you select and writes converted images to the output location you choose. It does not upload your images, filenames, folder paths, or image metadata to akki.lu or an analytics service.

Appearance, conversion preferences, and related settings are saved locally. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, usage analytics, installation tracking, or crash-report uploads.

## Updates and website links

The Microsoft Store version receives updates through Microsoft Store. It does not use the separate akki.lu update service. Microsoft's services are governed by Microsoft's privacy policies and your Windows and Store settings.

The version downloaded directly from GitHub can contact `update.akki.lu` to check for and download updates, according to your update preference. Like other web connections, these requests expose connection information such as your IP address to the hosting service. They do not include your images or a persistent analytics installation identifier.

Opening the website or support links launches a separate browser or app. Those websites and services have their own privacy policies. If you choose to submit a public GitHub issue, its content is visible publicly.

## Contact

For questions about the app or this policy, visit [akki.lu](https://akki.lu) or [open an issue in the project repository](https://github.com/nunolu/akki-image-converter/issues). Do not include private images or personal information in a public issue.
